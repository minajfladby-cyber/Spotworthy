How spotworthy works

A quick guide to getting started and using the app.

Everything you need to find your next favorite spot

Follow people you trust

Follow friends, family and influencers, and see their reviews together in one clear feed.

Reviews as polaroids

Every post is a real photo you took yourself, a star rating and a few words. No long, cluttered texts.

See it on the map

Open the Map tab to see a pink pin for every place you and your friends have reviewed. Tap a pin to read the reviews.

Get started in 3 steps

1

Create an account

Sign up with your name, email and a username. You get a confirmation link by email, but you can use the app right away.

2

Follow people

Search for friends by name or username. Friend requests must be approved unless the profile is public.

3

Discover and share

Tap + at the bottom of the app to post your own polaroid review with stars, a short text and a place, either public or friends only.
